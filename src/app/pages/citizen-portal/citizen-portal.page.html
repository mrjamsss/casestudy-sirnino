Fee Structure Guide

New Business Permit: Base: ₱5,000 + 1% of capital
Business Permit Renewal: Base: ₱2,500 + 0.5% of capital
Building Permit: 2% of project cost
Sanitary Permit: Flat: ₱500

Additional Fees

  • Mayor's Permit: Included in business permit
  • Fire Safety Inspection: ₱500-2,000
  • Health Certificate: ₱200 per employee
  • Barangay Clearance: ₱50-100
  • Community Tax Certificate: ₱100-500
